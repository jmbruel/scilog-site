## Résumé

Designing a cyber-physical system involves many heterogeneous models: CAD
geometry, simulations, control models, software. Each describes one aspect of
the system in its own formalism, yet all of them must describe the same
system. Keeping them consistent can be seen as an interoperability problem:
models written in different languages must be related, often without any way of
translating one into another. In this talk, I will present joint work with
colleagues at KIT on formal foundations for model consistency. I will introduce
a semantic account of consistency and our notion of observables: measurable
system properties that act as a shared interface between heterogeneous models. I
will also discuss how these ideas let existing formal-methods tools (solvers,
model checkers, etc.) help detect and repair inconsistencies, and end with
questions on how proof and analysis tools could help support consistency
management.
