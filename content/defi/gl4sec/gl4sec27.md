This **scientific workshop** brings together French and international researchers working at the **intersection of software engineering and cybersecurity**. Its objective is to foster discussions on how **testing**, **verification**, and other software engineering methods can be leveraged to improve the security, reliability, and trustworthiness of software systems. The program spans both foundational techniques and recent advances, covering established research directions as well as emerging topics and challenges.

*The workshop is co-organized by the [GL4Sec](/defi/gl4sec) challenge, the [MTV2](/group/mtv2) and [GLSec](/group/gl-sec) working groups of the GdR [SciLog](/), and the [SECUBIC](https://secubic-ptcc.github.io) project of the [PTCC](https://ptcc.fr/projets/secubic/). We gratefully acknowledge the support of the CNRS, the PTCC, and the CEA.*
