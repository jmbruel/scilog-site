Software Engineering and Security Day 2027

This scientific workshop brings together French and international researchers working at the intersection of software engineering and cybersecurity. Its objective is to foster discussions on how testing, verification, and other software engineering methods can be leveraged to improve the security, reliability, and trustworthiness of software systems. The program spans both foundational techniques and recent advances, covering established research directions as well as emerging topics and challenges.

The workshop is co-organized by the GL4Sec challenge, the MTV2 and GLSec working groups of the GdR SciLog, and the SECUBIC project of the PTCC. We gratefully acknowledge the support of the CNRS, the PTCC, and the CEA.

Practical info

  • When? January 7, 2027 from 10:00 to 17:00
  • Where? Campus Cyber Paris-La Défense, 5-7 Rue Bellini, 92800 Puteaux, France
  • How to get there?
    • Metro line 1: stop at Esplanade de la Défense, walk 3 minutes
    • RER lines A or E: stop at La Défense Grande Arche, take Metro line 1 or walk 15 minutes

Registration is free but mandatory before December 7, 2026 using the dedicated form.
